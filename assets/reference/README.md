# Approved visual direction

`art-direction.png` is a concept study generated with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-09-15. Kyle selected: “Much closer — use this as the visual target.” The original remains in the Codex generated-images directory. This copy is a design reference and is not loaded into the game or presented as a gameplay screenshot.

## Generation brief

An original stylized 3D evolution and god-simulation world titled A LITTLE TOO ALIVE, labeled ART DIRECTION STUDY. One coherent lush alien planet with continents, oceans, rivers, forests, cliffs and an atmospheric horizon. A foreground family herd has articulated anatomy, expressive eyes, varied horns, fins, tails and feet, with a juvenile visibly inheriting traits from both parents and a nearby stalking predator. An evolving civilization harvests and carries resources. A huge walking living city combines inhabited terraced architecture, bridges, towers, gardens, flesh, bark and coral on a many-legged organism. Orbital and individual-creature insets communicate camera scale. Sophisticated sculpted forms, rich material variation, cinematic natural light and organic surrealism; the wonder of an evolution sandbox such as Spore with original creatures and architecture. Avoid copying existing characters or interfaces, tiny floating islands, simple geometric blobs, dashboard UI, excessive text, gore and sexual imagery.

## Practical status

The v0.2 procedural browser build establishes the planet camera and several connected life systems. Its fidelity is substantially below this concept image. The image is a direction for future art work, not a performance or feature guarantee.
